What is TORQX AUTOCARE?

State Integrity

12-State Service State Machine

TORQX strictly forbids illegal state jumps. A job cannot be marked COMPLETED without passing through QUALITY_CHECK and PAYMENT.

1. BOOKING_CONFIRMED
Bay reserved. Awaiting vehicle arrival at garage.
2. VEHICLE_RECEIVED
Car enters shop. Job Card created. Bay occupied.
3. INSPECTION
Technician executes 60-point digital checklist.
4. DIAGNOSIS
Fault codes logged. Scope of work finalized.
5. QUOTE_PENDING
Quotation draft generated with parts & labour.
6. APPROVAL_PENDING
Customer reviewing digital estimate on portal.
7. PARTS_RESERVED
Inventory stock locked to job card to prevent shortage.
8. WORK_IN_PROGRESS
Technician actively servicing and replacing parts.
9. QUALITY_CHECK
Inspector conducts road test and 10-point audit.
10. READY_FOR_DELIVERY
Vehicle detailed. Bay released. Invoice payable.
11. OUT_FOR_DELIVERY
Valet driver en route to customer doorstep.
12. COMPLETED
Payment settled. Warranty active. Record archived.

Technical Defense

Technical Interview Questions & Answers

Deep architectural rationale for engineering interviews:

How do you handle concurrent booking race conditions for the same service bay? +
In the planned PostgreSQL backend, service bay reservations use pessimistic row-level locking (SELECT ... FOR UPDATE) wrapped inside an ACID transaction. If two customers attempt to book the same bay and time slot simultaneously, the second transaction is blocked until the first commits, automatically returning an HTTP 409 Conflict with next available slot suggestions.
Why implement a centralized State Machine instead of simple status updates? +
Automotive garages involve financial liability and physical safety. A state machine enforces business invariables: for example, a vehicle cannot be marked 'WORK_IN_PROGRESS' if an inspection uncovered critical defects requiring customer quotation approval. Bypassing steps would lead to unauthorized repairs, billing disputes, or uninspected vehicles on public roads.
How does inventory stock reservation differ from stock consumption? +
When a technician adds a part (e.g. Brembo brake pads) to a job card, the system immediately reserves that stock (reservedQuantity += 1). This prevents other bays from allocating the same shelf item while waiting for the customer's quote sign-off. If the customer rejects the work, the reservation is released. Once the vehicle is delivered, the stock is physically consumed (stockQuantity -= 1, reservedQuantity -= 1).
